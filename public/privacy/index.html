Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

BG Remover (removebg.sbs) is a free tool that removes image backgrounds directly in your browser. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Your images stay on your device

Images you choose are processed locally in your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers and we do not keep copies of them. To work, the tool downloads its AI model files (about 4 MB for the standard model and about 25 MB for the optional Portrait HD model) and keeps them in your browser storage so they load faster next time.

Android app

The BG Remover app on Google Play (package sbs.removebg.app) opens this same website inside Chrome on your phone. It requests no extra Android permissions, and everything on this page applies to the app in the same way. Images you pick from your gallery are processed on your phone and are never uploaded.

What is stored on your device

  • Your light or dark theme choice, saved in local storage.
  • Cached app files and AI models, so the tool can work offline after the first visit.

You can remove all of this at any time by clearing the site data in your browser settings.

Information we collect

You do not need an account, and we do not run our own analytics or tracking scripts. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may process standard technical request data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites. Ads are never shown inside the image tools, and your images are never shared with advertisers.

  • You can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in regions that require consent may see a consent message provided by Google before ads that use cookies are shown.

External links

This site links to other websites such as Trakteer, GitHub, and the author's portfolio. They have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

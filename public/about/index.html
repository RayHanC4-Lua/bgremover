About BG Remover

A free background remover that runs in your browser.

BG Remover removes the background from photos without sending them anywhere. You open the page, choose an image, and the cutout is made on your own device.

How it works

A small AI model runs inside your browser using ONNX Runtime Web. The standard model is lightweight so it stays quick on phones. For photos of people there is an optional Portrait HD mode that uses a larger model for finer hair detail. After the first visit the models are cached, so the tool also works offline once installed as an app.

What you can do

  • Remove a background and download a transparent PNG, or export JPG and WebP.
  • Replace the background with a color, gradient, blurred copy, or your own image.
  • Fix details with an erase and restore brush, and compare with the original.
  • Resize for square, portrait, story, wide, and ID photo formats.
  • Add a soft shadow or a sticker-style outline.

Open source and credits

The code is open source under the MIT License on GitHub. It builds on U2-Net (Apache License 2.0), MODNet (Apache License 2.0) with an ONNX export from the Xenova/modnet repository on Hugging Face, and ONNX Runtime Web (MIT License).

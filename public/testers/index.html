Become a BG Remover tester

Help me publish BG Remover on Google Play. Google requires 12 people to test a new app for 14 days before it can go public. It only takes a minute.

  1. 1

    Sign up below

    Use the Google account email that is signed in to Play Store on your phone.

  2. 2

    Join the test

    Once you are added, open the test link and tap Become a tester.

  3. 3

    Keep it for 14 days

    Install the app from Google Play and keep it installed. Using it now and then is a bonus.

Your email is only used to add you to the test on Google Play and is deleted after the app is public. See the Privacy Policy.

You are on the list

Open test link

Frequently asked questions

The things people ask most.

Is it really free?

Yes. Processing runs on your device, so server costs are close to zero. If the tool helps you, you can support it on Trakteer.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser. You can verify this by using the tool in airplane mode after the page has loaded.

Why is the result sometimes imperfect?

The tool uses a lightweight model so it stays fast on phones. Very fine hair, see-through objects, or low-contrast images can come out less than perfect. Photos with a clear subject give the best results.

Which formats and sizes are supported?

JPG, PNG, and WebP. The longest side is limited to 2048 pixels to keep processing smooth, and results are downloaded as PNG.

Why is the first run slower?

The AI model, about 4 MB, is downloaded once and stored on your device. After that, processing is much faster.
